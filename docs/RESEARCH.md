# Research Foundations

## YC RFS alignment

[The Primer (YC RFS)](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs) asks for an adaptive tutor that teaches young children to read, write, and do arithmetic at private-tutor quality.

Lumi’s wedge: **neurodivergent-first Primer** for autistic kids 7–10.

## Instructional evidence used

- Science of Reading sequence (phonemic awareness → phonics → fluency → vocabulary → comprehension)
- Computer-assisted reading instruction shows promising outcomes for many autistic learners (systematic reviews; programs in the ABRACADABRA/Headsprout evidence class)
- Math EBPs for ASD: virtual manipulatives, modified schema-based instruction, explicit instruction, least-to-most prompting, graphic organizers
- Writing: Cover-Copy-Compare, sentence frames, SRSD-lite (POW), typing/speak as valid transcription paths, video modeling for letter formation
- Homework UX: visual schedules, First-Then, timers, special interests, sensory breaks, literal language
